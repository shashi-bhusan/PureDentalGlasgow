Welcome to Pure Dental Care

We are located in the South side of Glasgow, serving patients from this location for over 60 years. Recently, we have changed our name from Rowlands Dental Care to Pure Dental Care and continue to deliver the same high quality of care.

All our treatments are as per the GDC guidelines and we take great pride in taking the preventative approach to dentistry. Our team of dentists use the latest in technology and equipment and undertake a range of treatments, from regular dental care to advanced dentistry. We revel in creating great customer service experience at every step of your interaction with our practice. Please do get in touch with us for any of your queries.
